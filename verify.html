Official Certificate Verification

Verify your LGES certificate

Scan the QR code printed on the certificate, or enter the Certificate ID manually. Verification is checked against the LGES certificate registry.

Scan QR Code

Allow camera access and place the certificate QR code inside the scanner frame.

How verification works

The QR code contains an LGES verification URL. Older certificates that contain only the Certificate ID are also supported.

1
ScanUse your phone camera to scan the QR printed on the certificate.
2
IdentifyThe page extracts the Certificate ID from the QR URL or scanned text.
3
Check registryThe ID is checked against the LGES cloud certificate registry.
4
View detailsMatching certificate details are shown so they can be compared with the printed certificate.
Security note: A valid result means the Certificate ID exists in the LGES registry. Always compare the displayed name, course, certificate type, session and date with the physical certificate.
